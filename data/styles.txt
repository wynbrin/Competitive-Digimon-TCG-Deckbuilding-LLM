# data/styles.txt — strategic STYLE axes, orthogonal to archetype.
#
# A style describes *how* a deck wins, not *which* cards it runs. A single
# archetype can be built in several styles; a deck gets a continuous score in
# [0,1] for EVERY style (styles are non-exclusive), computed by
# scripts/classify_styles.py.
#
# Two kinds of style, distinguished by the first field:
#
#   text       | Name : signal, signal, ...
#       Signals are phrases matched against card EFFECT text
#       (main_effect + source_effect + alt_effect). A deck's score = the
#       fraction of its card copies whose effect text hits any signal.
#       This is the community-knowledge part: you encode "what an effect that
#       does X reads like." Phrases are normalized the same way card text is
#       (lowercased, punctuation collapsed), so write them plainly — e.g.
#       "your opponent's hand" becomes "your opponent s hand". Multi-word
#       phrases are fine and are matched as a whole.
#
#   structural | Name :
#       No signals. The score is computed from deck COMPOSITION (curve, levels,
#       copy counts) by a heuristic coded in classify_styles.py. The line still
#       lives here so the style has a stable slug/name and shows up in the views.
#
# Lines starting with '#' and blank lines are ignored. Edit this file, then:
#   python -m ingestion.loaders.ingestion styles
#   python scripts/classify_styles.py
#
# These starter signals are deliberately rough — eyeball the classifier output
# and tune the phrases before expanding the taxonomy.

# Styles are grouped into TIERS with "# @category <name>" directives below.
# A directive applies to every style after it until the next one. Categories are
# free text (stored on each style) — rename or add freely. Current tiers:
#   disruption — interferes with the opponent's resources/plan
#   build      — structural deck shape (composition, not effect text)
#   resource   — engines that generate your own card/security advantage
#   engine     — recurring mechanical engines that aren't a strategic axis
#   misc       — default for any style with no @category above it

# @category disruption

# Strips the opponent's digivolution sources / cards out from under their stacks.
text | Source Stripping : trash the bottom digivolution card, digivolution card of 1 of your opponent

# Attacks the opponent's hand.
text | Hand Disruption : from your opponent s hand, your opponent trashes, reveal your opponent s hand, opponent trashes 1 card

# Disrupts the opponent's Tamers (delete / bounce / lock). Action verbs only:
# the bare "your opponent s tamers" also catches "for each ..."-style conditions.
text | Tamer Hate : delete 1 of your opponent s tamers, return 1 of your opponent s tamers, your opponent s tamers can t, your opponent s tamers don t unsuspend

# Manipulates / mills the opponent's security stack.
text | Security Manipulation : trash the top card of your opponent s security, top card of your opponent s security, reveal the top card of your opponent s security

# Efficient/mass removal and tempo denial (delete, de-digivolve, bounce).
text | Removal Control : de digivolve, delete 1 of your opponent s, by deleting, return 1 of your opponent s, to the bottom of, delete all, suspend all of your opponent s, suspend 1 of your opponent s

# Prevents your opponent's resources from suspending/unsuspending (suspend-state
# lock). Attack prevention ("can't attack or block") lives in Combat Denial.
text | Stun : can t suspend, can t unsuspend

# @category resource

# Manipulates both players' hand.
text | Hand Advantage : Both players draw, for every 4 cards in your opponent s hand, when an effect adds a card, for every 4 cards in your hand

# Manipulates / mills your own security stack for value.
text | Security As Resource : recovery, top card of your security, to your security stack, search your security

# @category engine

# Builds value/board by placing cards under permanents (DigiXros requirements,
# source-loading, Tamer-fueling). Broad, Xros-heavy mechanic tag rather than a
# strategic axis — hence the 'engine' tier.
text | Placement Engine : by placing, under one of your, under this digimon

# Self-discard as cost/fuel (trash cards from YOUR OWN hand to pay/power effects).
# Distinct from Hand Disruption, which trashes the OPPONENT's hand. Leans purple
# (Cerberus, Leviamon, Fenriloogamon, Belphemon). Action phrasings only — the
# bare "cards in your hand" also catches "for each card in your hand" conditions.
text | Trash from Hand : trash 1 card in your hand, trashing 1 card in your hand, trash 2 cards in your hand

# Frontier spirit-evolution engine: deck built on Hybrid-form Digimon (played /
# digivolved off a Tamer, not climbed). Scored structurally off the card `form`
# field in classify_styles.py (cleaner than effect text); the ':' text is ignored.
structural | Hybrid :

# Armor digivolution engine: deck built on Armor-form Digimon (digivolved with a
# Digi-Egg). Structural, off the card `form` field (= 'Armor Form'). Leans on
# Magnamon/Armor Rush and the Imperial decks.
structural | Armor :

# DNA Digivolution (Jogress) engine: digivolve by combining two Digimon. There is
# no DNA `form`, so this reads the "DNA digivolve" mechanic from effect text.
text | DNA Digivolution : dna digivolve, dna digivolving

# X Antibody engine: deck built on [X Antibody]-trait Digimon (X-evolution), a
# tribal-style mechanic spanning many color lines. Structural, off digi_type.
# X decks usually also score Tall Stack (they climb a line) — that overlap is
# expected; the X engine is the defining axis, not the stack shape.
structural | X Antibody :

# @category defense

# Protects your own Digimon: effect immunity, Save (survives deletion), and
# surviving battle. (Effect-deletion immunity "by your opponent s effects" is the
# umbrella; it also covers the rarer "isn t affected by your opponent s effects".)
text | Resilience : by your opponent s effects, it isn t deleted, can t be deleted in battle

# Stops or redirects the opponent's attacks: Blocker walls, defensive <Decoy>
# (redirect to THIS Digimon, not the offensive <Raid> "to 1 of your opponent"),
# and outright attack denial. Scored in code (COMBAT_DENIAL_SIGNALS in
# classify_styles.py) so Blocker — a near-staple keyword — can be weighted below
# the rarer, more intentional denial effects.
structural | Combat Denial :
